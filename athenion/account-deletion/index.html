Athenion – Account Deletion

Information on how to request deletion of your Athenion account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Athenion account using one of the following methods:

From within the app
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account

Via email
Send a request to: support@vbverse.com

Please include your registered email ID in the deletion request so we can identify your account.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (email, login details)
  • Tasks and projects
  • Time tracking logs
  • Reports and analytics data

What Data May Be Retained

  • Minimal logs for legal and security purposes, retained for up to 30 days

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 days.

Important Notes

  • If you are using external integrations such as Jira or Todoist, those systems are not affected by Athenion account deletion.
  • API keys provided by users are stored locally on the device and are not retained after account deletion.
Note: If account-related data exists only on your device in local storage, deleting your account or app data may also remove that information from the device, depending on your usage and app state.
For any questions regarding account deletion, contact support@vbverse.com.
